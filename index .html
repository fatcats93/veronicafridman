ABOUT ME

A filmmaker first. A storyteller always.

My passion for filmmaking began in 2020 when I started creating stories with whatever resources I had available. Since then I've directed narrative films, managed production crews, designed sets, written screenplays, and explored storytelling through animation, comics, journalism, and theatre.

I've studied filmmaking through the New York Film Academy and Columbia University's Pre-College Program, where I directed multiple short films while collaborating with talented casts and crews. My goal is to create films that entertain audiences while exploring meaningful ideas about people and the world around them.
