ABOUT ME

ABOUT ME

Ever since I was little, I have loved art and storytelling, but my passion for film making began in 2020 during quarantine when my “Quaranteam” and I created a reality TV show starring ourselves.

Since then, I have continued making films with whatever resources I had—from stop-motion animations using Among Us figures to convincing my brother to become a part-time actor, to directing a crew of 5+ at NYFA and Columbia University’s Pre-College Program.

My favorite genres to watch and create are comedy and horror, and many of my films combine the two. My goal as a filmmaker is to make audiences scream and laugh at the same time. I hope to continue creating stories that entertain people throughout high school, college, and the rest of my life.

Alongside filmmaking, I have developed a love for history and journalism, which has influenced the way I approach storytelling. I want to learn how to incorporate these interests into my films by creating stories with more meaning, and connections to the world around me.
